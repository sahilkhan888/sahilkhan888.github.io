Being bored helps us get into the default network mode where we are involved in self reflection, wandering thoughts and in our headspace

Phones today snatches this mode from us, in-fact we are getting more and more further away from getting to this mode as we are too much into our devices. this affects our thinking, leading to more chaos

it is better to limit the phone to a certain time of the day and engage in something physical like reading a book or just embracing boredom and your thoughts
